## 🔍 Conceptual Exploration
[cite_start]We explored several real-world problems based on our personal experiences[cite: 495]:
1.  [cite_start]**Accessible Academic (Selected):** Solving the lack of accurate Hebrew captions and summaries in academic VODs [cite: 496-503].
2.  [cite_start]**RoboTrack:** A pedagogical dashboard for robotics education [cite: 504-510].
3.  [cite_start]**GroupScribe:** Live speaker diarization for noisy social settings [cite: 511-523].

## 🎯 Why Accessible Academic?
[cite_start]We chose this concept because it addresses an acute social and pedagogical gap in the Israeli academic market[cite: 503]. [cite_start]Current solutions like YouTube Auto-CC are often inaccurate for Hebrew technical terms, creating a significant barrier for hearing-impaired students[cite: 497, 531].
