# 💡 Teaming & Ideation: The Genesis of Accessible Academic

This folder documents the initial phase of our project within the **Software Entrepreneurship Workshop 2025**. It outlines our team's core competencies and the selection process that led to our final concept.

## 👥 The Team
Our team consists of two Computer Science students from MTA, combining technical skills with a deep personal connection to the problem of accessibility.

* **Adi Tapiro:** Technical lead with professional experience in robotics instruction and a unique perspective as a hearing-impaired student.
* **Matan Gurfinkel:** User-experience and logistics lead, focusing on the challenges of remote learning and time management.

## 🔍 Conceptual Exploration
We explored several real-world problems based on our personal experiences:
1. **Accessible Academic (Selected):** Solving the lack of accurate Hebrew captions and summaries in academic VODs.
2. **RoboTrack:** A pedagogical dashboard for robotics education.
3. **GroupScribe:** Live speaker diarization for noisy social settings.

## 🎯 Why Accessible Academic?
We chose this concept because it addresses an acute social and pedagogical gap in the Israeli academic market. Current solutions like YouTube Auto-CC are often inaccurate for Hebrew technical terms, creating a significant barrier for hearing-impaired students.
