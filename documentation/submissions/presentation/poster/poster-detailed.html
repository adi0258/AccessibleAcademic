01 The problem Recorded lectures are now a primary source of knowledge — and a barrier
01

Captions fail in Hebrew

Automatic captioning is unreliable for Hebrew, and collapses on the technical vocabulary of mathematics, physics and computer science — exactly where precision matters most.

02

Hall audio is unusable

Large rooms and low-grade microphones produce recordings that defeat both the listener and the speech-recognition engine.

03

Hours of video, no structure

Students re-watch entire lectures to find one derivation, and build summaries by hand from material they could not fully hear.

04 What makes it different Two problems no general transcription tool solves
Original contribution

A grounding pass that refuses to invent

A study aid that hallucinates is worse than none. Before anything reaches the student, four agents audit the generated material against the transcript it came from.

Agent 1
Summary audit
Scores every sentence for support in the transcript.
Agent 2
Flashcard audit
Flags any question or answer without a source.
Agent 3
Confidence
Independent cross-check on the other two.
Agent 4
Purifier
Rewrites flagged items from the transcript, or removes them.
Agents 1–3 run concurrently; only the purified content is stored and exported.

Mathematics that survives every export

A formula is right-to-left inside right-to-left prose, and each output format speaks a different language. Three rendering paths, one source.

Source
v = \frac{u_1 - u_2}{2}
Web · KaTeX
\(v = \frac{u_1 - u_2}{2}\)
Word · OMML
\(v = \frac{u_1 - u_2}{2}\)
PDF · Unicode
v = (u₁ − u₂) ⁄ 2
PDFDOCX SRTVTT

The same LaTeX source drives all four. Word receives editable equations, not pictures of equations; the PDF writer falls back to a Unicode transcription so Hebrew and mathematics can share a line without breaking either.

Accessibility as the design, not a setting

Captions you control

Show and hide without losing sync. On restore, the caption for the current timestamp appears immediately — no rewind, no drift.

Direction follows the reader

The page sets its own language and reading direction from the system locale. Hebrew RTL is the default, not the fallback.

Word-level timing

Timestamps on individual words drive the player, the transcript and the subtitle files from one source of truth.

Leaves with the student

PDF and Word for revision, SRT and VTT for any player or learning-management system. Nothing is locked inside the product.

07 The interface

Right-to-left by default. Layout direction, labels and controls follow the reader’s system locale — Hebrew RTL by default, English LTR when the browser asks for it. One upload starts the entire pipeline; the lecture list reports each stage as it completes.
