Every student deserves to hear the lecture.

We turn unclear Hebrew lecture recordings into accurate captions and ready-to-use study material — automatically.

“For three years I sat with recordings I could not follow. No captions — or captions that got every technical word wrong.”
Adi Tapiero Computer science student, hearing impaired.
She built the tool she needed. It turned out everyone needed it.
Accessibility shouldn’t be a checkbox.
It should be a commitment.

How it works

Upload once. Everything else is automatic.
